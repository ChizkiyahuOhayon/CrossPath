\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method, a complete compatibility view of endpoint composition for composed image retrieval. Two trained endpoints define four retrieval paths, not two. Evaluating the off-diagonal paths reveals combinations of query understanding and gallery geometry that matched endpoint scores cannot express. A parameter-free cross mean improves strong heterogeneous systems on FashionIQ, while lightweight joint routing converts complementary early and deeper rank behavior into consistent FashionGen gains. Conservative fusion and target refinement extend the same principle to open-domain CIRR. The compute-matched diagonal comparison, directional matrix, and coordinate-scrambling intervention identify cross-endpoint compatibility as a distinct source of retrieval ability. This perspective is model agnostic and can accompany future advances in CIR endpoint design without changing their training objectives.
